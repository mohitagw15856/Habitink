Install HabitInk on your Xteink X3 / X4

HabitInk is a one-button habit tracker for the Xteink e-readers. It installs into the reader's spare firmware slot, so your stock reader firmware stays on the device and you can switch back any time. Hold Back for 1.5 s inside HabitInk to return to the reader; use the second button below to switch back from a computer.

Read before flashing.
  1. Works in Chrome or Edge on a computer (Web Serial). Use the cable that came with the device; on the X3 the magnetic cable must be the data-capable one.
  2. This writes firmware slot A and tells the bootloader to boot it. Your stock reader must be in slot B, which is the case once the reader has taken at least one stock update over Wi-Fi. If you are not sure, use the SD-card method below instead: the stock bootloader then chooses the slot itself.
  3. Some readers bought from third-party stores are USB-locked and will not show up in the port picker. Do not force it; use the SD-card method.
  4. Do not tick "erase device" if the flasher offers it.

1. Install HabitInk

Turn the reader on, plug it in, then:

Your browser does not support Web Serial. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.

After flashing, hold Power for two seconds if the screen stays blank. On first run HabitInk writes three starter habits to /.habitink/habits.tsv on the SD card; edit that file to make them yours.

2. Return to the stock reader

Only flips which slot boots; nothing is erased. HabitInk stays in slot A until you install something else there.

Your browser does not support Web Serial. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.

SD-card method (no cable, works on locked units)

  1. Download Habitink.bin and rename it to update.bin.
  2. Copy it to the root of the reader's SD card (FAT32 or exFAT).
  3. Insert the card, plug in power, and hold Power together with the top-left button until the update screen appears.
